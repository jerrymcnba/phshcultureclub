About the club

Our Story

Founded in 2024, Pinghe Shanghai Culture Club began with two students born and raised in Shanghai. Seeing how the local dialect and culture has been gradually fading in recent years, we wanted to do something to preserve them, starting with our own school.

Today, our club community has grown to over 120 members. Through 22 club events, we have introduced students to Shanghai's local culture and helped them learn more about Shanghainese through lessons and activities.

A familiar place, in another voice.
Looking ahead

Continuing the project

What you see on this website is only the beginning. I plan to keep expanding and improving it, even after I graduate from Pinghe.

Here are some directions I hope to explore in the future:

01Complete our existing word cardsFinish recording audio and digitizing the word cards we have already created.

02Expand our vocabulary collectionCreate more word cards covering different aspects of everyday life beyond the school campus, gradually building a small Shanghainese dictionary.

03Explore pronunciation differences across generationsI've noticed differences in how Shanghainese is pronounced by older speakers, including my grandparents and those of other club members, compared with younger generations. I hope to collect recordings from speakers of different ages, compare their pronunciations, and identify recurring patterns of variation.

04Develop beginner-friendly Shanghainese lessonsCreate a more structured introduction to Shanghainese, helping beginners understand the language and learn how to use it in everyday conversations.

05Share more about Shanghai's culture and historyIntroduce local traditions, customs, and how the city has changed over the years.
